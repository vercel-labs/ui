<Callout>

These docs cover ui-sdk in Angular. Using another framework? See [React](/docs/react), [Vue](/docs/vue), [Svelte](/docs/svelte), [Solid](/docs/solid) or [Web Components](/docs/web-components). To build your own components from behaviors, see [Core](/docs/core).

</Callout>

`@ui-sdk/angular` gives you components such as Select, Dialog, and Tabs, built on ui-sdk's behaviors. They handle interactions, focus, and ARIA attributes. You choose their markup and styles.

Each part is a directive you put on your own elements. Import the parts you need, add them to your component's `imports`, and style them with your own CSS.
