`@ui-sdk/vue` gives you components such as Select, Dialog, and Tabs, built on ui-sdk's behaviors. They handle interactions, focus, and ARIA attributes. You choose their markup and styles.

Import the parts you need, put them together in your app, and style them with your own CSS.
