An agent reaches and reads more of the page in one call. A `press` finds a button by its exact text in the open component that holds it, or by a row's name and the button's text. A name can carry its tab or section in front without `>`, like `"Email deploy failures"`. The snapshot ends with a `closed` entry naming the closed tabs and sections. An open dialog or popover lists its own text, an empty field lists its placeholder, and a row's text runs to 200 characters on its first component. `query` with `closed: true` alone shows the first 3 rows of each closed place and counts the rest, and `gone` stops at ten. When `allow` isn't answered in time, the error says the step didn't run and to tell the person.
