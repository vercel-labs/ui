An agent's step now finds its component on another tab, on another page of a pagination, or in a closed section or collapsible, and opens what it is behind. A place before the name, like `"Email > Deploy failures"`, picks between look-alikes, which carry a `section` in the snapshot. A tab or section inside `data-agent-confirm` isn't opened to look. A `set` step fails when its value didn't take, so a step that saves can go last in the same call. A step that names something that isn't there lists what the closed tabs and sections hold, and names an open modal that covers the page. `press` takes a near miss of a button's text, like `"Mark as paid"` for **Mark paid**, and a name can put a component around it first, like `"dialog > Status"`. An `expect` step checks a value, a checked state or a row's text without changing it, and stops the call when it differs. `query` takes `closed: true` to read the other tabs and closed sections in one call. A list with more than 10 items in its data now says how many it has, as `more` on its snapshot entry, and `query` takes `from` with `within` to read a list past its first 50 items. `act` now checks the shape of the whole call before any step runs: a step with a key other than `ref`, `action` and `args`, or `args` that isn't a list, fails with what to send. A number outside a component's `min` and `max` is refused, where it used to be clamped, and a number sent to a text field, or to a list whose values are text, is stored as text. The bridge carries `agentTools` as `agent.tools`. `agentTools` lists `act` first, with descriptions that plan a whole task in one call.
